# Landing Page Copy Review

Review page copy against Looply's copy conventions and suggest concrete improvements.

## Review Checklist

Go through every piece of copy on the page and check:

1. **Headline** -- does the `h1` describe a benefit for the visitor instead of naming the product? Weak: "Welcome to Looply". Strong: "Build tiny habits that stick".
2. **Specificity** -- is every claim concrete? Flag filler such as "a lot of features", "very good", or "and more".
3. **Call to action** -- is there exactly one primary action per page, with a descriptive label? Flag vague labels like "Click here" or "Learn more" when a specific label fits.
4. **Tone** -- encouraging and calm. Flag pressure or guilt framing ("Don't fail again!") and replace it with supportive language.
5. **Scannability** -- can a visitor understand the page from headings and highlighted text alone? Flag long paragraphs that hide the main point.

## Output Format

For each finding, report:

- The current text.
- Why it falls short (reference the checklist item).
- A suggested replacement.

End with a short summary of the overall copy quality and the three highest-impact improvements.
